About Me

I didn't arrive at software through a traditional path. For over 10 years I worked alongside my father in a metalworking shop, manufacturing and repairing machinery, while studying the core curriculum of an Engineering degree. Integrating automation with Ladder diagrams and PLCs in the shop led me into the world of 3D printing with my venture Emme 3D, and from there into web development: Argentina Programa, a QA Testing course, and self-directed study. Today I build complete full-stack applications with React, Node.js, Express, and PostgreSQL, using AI strategically (spec-driven development, model evaluation, context optimization).
